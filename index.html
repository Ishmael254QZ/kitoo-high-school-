About Our School

Empowering students for a bright future

Our Vision

To be a center of academic excellence that nurtures holistic development and produces responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through innovative teaching methods, modern facilities, and a supportive environment that enables students to achieve their full potential.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Discipline, Innovation, and Respect - the core values that guide our daily interactions and educational practices.

School Management System

Comprehensive digital solutions for modern education

Admission Module

Online application system with document upload and automated admission number generation.

Student Registration

Complete student profile management, class assignment, and guardian contact records.

Staff Management

Teacher and non-teaching staff records, payroll, attendance tracking, and role assignment.

Library Management

Digital catalog, book issuance, return tracking, and automated overdue reminders.

Academics Module

Timetable generation, subject management, teacher allocation, and lesson attendance.

Exam & Results

Automated grading, performance analytics, report generation, and KCSE grading scale integration.

Fees & Finance

Payment tracking, receipt generation, M-Pesa integration, bursary management, and SMS reminders.

Hostel/Boarding

Dormitory assignment, attendance tracking, house master reports, and inventory management.

Inventory Management

Asset tracking, textbook inventory, stationery distribution, and maintenance reports.

Communication

SMS/Email alerts, notices to parents, announcements, and circular distribution.

Discipline Module

Misconduct tracking, prefect reports, punishment records, and suspension management.

Student & Parent Portals

Personalized dashboards for students and parents to access information and communicate.
