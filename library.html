School Library

Your Gateway to Knowledge and Learning

Welcome to Our Library

A school library is an essential learning resource center designed to support students and teachers by providing access to books, information, and study materials. It promotes reading, research skills, and academic growth in a quiet, organized environment.

Our library is equipped with a comprehensive collection of books, digital resources, and modern facilities to enhance your learning experience. Whether you're looking for reference materials, fiction books, or academic resources, our library is here to serve you.

How the School Library Works

Everything you need to know about using our library

1

Registration

Students are registered in the library system using either a library card or their student ID. This allows them to borrow books and access library services. Registration is done automatically when you enroll in the school.

2

Searching for Books

Students can find books through:

  • Browsing the shelves arranged by subjects or categories
  • Using the library's digital catalog to search by:
    • Title
    • Author
    • Subject
    • Keywords

Book Organization:

  • Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC)
  • Separate Fiction and Non-Fiction sections
3

Borrowing Books

To borrow a book:

  1. Present the book and your library ID at the circulation desk
  2. The librarian records the book in the system
  3. You receive a due date for returning the book

Borrowing Rules:

  • Limited number of books per student
  • 1–2 week borrowing period
  • Renewals allowed if the book is not reserved
4

Returning Books

Books must be returned on or before the due date. Returns can be made at:

  • Circulation desk
  • Library drop box

Late returns may result in:

  • Fines
  • Temporary suspension from borrowing
  • Reminders sent to the student
5

Library Facilities

The library provides several spaces and resources:

Quiet Reading Area

Study Tables

Group Discussion Zones

Computer/Resource Area

Reference Section

Common Uses:

  • Revision
  • Research
  • Completing assignments
  • Accessing academic materials
6

Reference Services

Librarians assist users with:

  • Finding information
  • Locating materials
  • Using reference books (dictionaries, atlases, encyclopedias)
  • Learning research and citation skills

Reference books must be used inside the library only.

7

Library Rules

To maintain order and protect resources, students must follow these rules:

Maintain silence
Handle books carefully
No eating or drinking
No writing in books
Return books on time
8

Digital Services

Modern school libraries may offer:

E-books

Online Journals

Digital Catalog Search

Wi-Fi

Educational Databases

Learning Platforms

Library Hours

Monday - Friday 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Saturday 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM
Sunday Closed
